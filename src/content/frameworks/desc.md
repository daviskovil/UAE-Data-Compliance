## What it is

DESC's Information Security Regulation is Dubai's mandatory information security
standard. It defines a control framework and a compliance and audit cycle for
Dubai Government and its supply chain.

## Who it applies to

- Dubai Government entities and their affiliates.
- Vendors and service providers delivering to Dubai Government.
- Systems that connect into Dubai Government networks.

## Key obligations

- Adopt the ISR control framework.
- Complete the DESC compliance assessment and audit cycle.
- Remediate findings within DESC timelines.
- Report incidents to DESC.

## Penalties and consequences

Contract loss, mandated remediation, and escalation within Dubai Government.

## How vendors help

Consultancies with DESC experience run the assessment and audit preparation;
managed providers sustain the controls.
