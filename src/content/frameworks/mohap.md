## What it is

The UAE's health data law regulates electronic health information: how it is
created, stored, shared between providers, and moved across borders. A central
health information system underpins it, and the law restricts where the data
can live.

## Who it applies to

- Licensed healthcare facilities of every kind.
- Insurers and administrators processing claims data.
- Digital health and telehealth companies serving UAE patients.

## Key obligations

- Keep UAE health data inside the UAE unless an exception is granted.
- Connect to and share data with the national/emirate health information
  platforms as required.
- Restrict access to health data to authorised purposes and people.
- Obtain patient consent for uses beyond direct care.
- Retain records for the mandated periods.

## Penalties and consequences

Administrative fines, licensing action by the health authority, and criminal
exposure for unlawful disclosure of health data.

## How vendors help

Specialist advisers handle residency exceptions and health-authority
submissions; UAE data centres provide compliant hosting; health-tech
integrators handle the platform connectivity.
