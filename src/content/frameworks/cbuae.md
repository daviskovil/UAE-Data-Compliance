## What it is

A set of CBUAE regulations and standards - covering outsourcing, cloud
computing, operational risk and consumer protection - that together shape how
licensed financial institutions handle data.

## Who it applies to

Every institution licensed by the CBUAE: banks, finance companies, insurers,
payment providers, and exchange houses.

## Key obligations

- Notify or obtain no-objection before material outsourcing and cloud moves.
- Keep core systems and customer data resilient and, in practice, in-country.
- Maintain exit and business-continuity plans for outsourced services.
- Report significant incidents to CBUAE within set timeframes.
- Hold the board and senior management accountable for compliance.

## Penalties and consequences

Fines, restrictions on business lines, forced remediation, and individual
accountability for senior managers.

## How vendors help

Advisers handle the CBUAE notification packs and outsourcing assessments;
UAE-based data centres and cloud providers supply the in-country hosting.
