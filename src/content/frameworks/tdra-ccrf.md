## What it is

TDRA's Cloud Computing Regulatory Framework is the UAE's rulebook for cloud
services. It defines how providers must operate, how customer data is
classified, and where different classes of data may be stored and processed.

## Who it applies to

- Cloud service providers marketing services in the UAE.
- Government and semi-government bodies buying cloud.
- Regulated-sector organisations whose data carries residency conditions.

## Key obligations

- Classify data according to the framework's sensitivity tiers.
- Store and process each class only in permitted locations.
- Use providers that meet the framework's operational and security controls.
- Maintain the audit evidence procurement teams will ask for.

## Penalties and consequences

Consequences are mostly commercial and regulatory: ineligibility for government
and regulated contracts, procurement rejections, and audit findings.

## How vendors help

Cloud providers and data centres demonstrate conformity; consultants handle
data classification, residency mapping, and the procurement paperwork.
