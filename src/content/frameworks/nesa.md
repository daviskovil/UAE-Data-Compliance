## What it is

A mandatory set of information assurance controls for UAE government bodies and
operators of Critical Information Infrastructure. The standards group controls
into management and technical families and assign each a priority tier.

## Who it applies to

- Federal government entities.
- Designated Critical Information Infrastructure operators.
- Their suppliers and contractors, where obligations are passed down.

## Key obligations

- Run a risk assessment against the IA control set.
- Implement the mandatory controls for your priority tier.
- Maintain a governance structure with named accountability.
- Report and manage incidents through the required channels.
- Evidence continuous compliance, not a one-off assessment.

## Penalties and consequences

Loss of authorisation to run critical services, regulator escalation, and
removal from government and critical-infrastructure supply chains.

security providers operate the ongoing controls and reporting.
