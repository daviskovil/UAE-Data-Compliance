Plenty of UAE businesses treated the PDPL as "GDPR, but here" and copied their
existing programme across. That gets you most of the way, but three gaps keep
showing up:

1. **Cross-border transfers.** The mechanisms are not identical to the GDPR's.
   Standard contractual clauses drafted for the EU are not automatically valid.
2. **Free-zone confusion.** Companies with a DIFC or ADGM entity sometimes apply
   the wrong regime, or assume the federal PDPL covers activity that is actually
   under the free-zone law.
3. **DPO triggers.** The thresholds for when a Data Protection Officer is
   mandatory differ, and "we have a GDPR DPO already" does not answer the
   question.

If any of these sound familiar, the [PDPL explainer](/frameworks/pdpl) walks
through the obligations, and the [directory](/directory) lists advisers who do
this work.
